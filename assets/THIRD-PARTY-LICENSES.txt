Minecraft texture assets

Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 block, selected entity and end-sky textures

Texture artwork copyright Mojang AB and Microsoft. All rights reserved by the
respective rights holders. The Minecraft texture assets and their generated
atlas are separate from this project's MIT-licensed code and Three.js.

Source: official Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 client archive. Includes block
texture PNGs and selected entity sheets for chests, banners and their patterns,
shulker boxes, decorated pots, bells, conduits, built-in mob/player heads,
the enchanting-table book, the end-portal surface, and the end-sky background,
with accompanying animation metadata. Exact entity directories and individual
files are recorded in source.json. Beds and signs use the 26.3 block sheets.
Original file bytes are preserved as base64 in texture-source/26.3.json.
Entity/environment files use entity/ and environment/ namespaced keys;
block filenames retain their original
paths relative to assets/minecraft/textures/block/. No external player skins
are fetched or included.
